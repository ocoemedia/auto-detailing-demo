ABOUT US • COMPLETELY FACTUAL HISTORY

Founded After a Pigeon Incident in 2019.

Example Site Auto Detailing began in late 2019 after our founder, Barnaby "Squeegee" Henderson, parked his prized 1998 Honda Civic under a suspiciously crowded oak tree for eleven minutes. Furious at nature and armed with three microfiber towels, a garden hose, and an unreasonable amount of energy drinks, he accidentally polished the clear coat straight down to the primer—and realized he might actually be onto something.

After learning how actual paint chemistry works (and legally changing his middle name to Ceramic), Barnaby built a mobile rig capable of turning muddy daily drivers into rolling mirrors. Today, we still haven't forgiven that pigeon, which is why we take personal revenge on dirt, brake dust, and swirl marks across your driveway.
